PRIVACY POLICY

Know what goes where.

How this website and the Kindred desktop handle information.

Effective and last updated: November 8, 2026 · Revision 2026-11-08

1. Visiting this website

The website is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting. The supporter portal development preview uses Firebase Authentication for optional Google or email/password accounts and a separate authenticated service for device connections. Current public Kindred downloads cannot connect to it yet. The website does not provide payment forms or advertising; its own code does not add analytics trackers, advertising cookies or external fonts. It requests release information from this website to show current downloads. Bigger text and stronger contrast preferences are saved in your browser’s local storage and can be cleared with browser site data.

Hosting and download providers receive connection information such as your IP address, requested URL, time of request and browser or device information to deliver content and operate and protect their services. Downloads and release links open GitHub. Provider logging, retention and processing are governed by their own policies: Google and GitHub. Kindred does not set those retention periods.

2. Download acknowledgment

The download page uses unchecked boxes to record your choices in the current page’s memory. This version does not save the acknowledgment in cookies or browser storage, send it to a Kindred consent database, or create an account. Reloading the page clears the choices. Checking that you have read this policy is an acknowledgment, not consent to advertising or unrelated processing.

Supporter portal preview

Signing in sends your account information to Firebase Authentication and, for Google sign-in, Google. This can include your email address, display name, provider identity and authentication records. Email passwords are submitted directly to Firebase Authentication by its SDK, not to the Kindred management API. The portal uses session persistence; sign out when you finish, especially on a shared computer.

The development management service stores your supporter identity, the device name and platform, its public signing key, approved actions, approval and expiration times, last contact, requested actions and reported results. Setup codes are stored as keyed hashes and expire after ten minutes. The service accepts only a public signing key; it does not receive device private keys. Device documents, calendar notes, AI provider keys, microphone recordings and screen contents are not sent to this management service.

Pairing requires approval on both screens. Either person can revoke a connection. Revocation blocks further authorization and does not undo actions already accepted for execution. Connections expire after 90 days. The portal shows recent activity from the last 90 days, with old activity and temporary security records scheduled for deletion using database retention policies. Provider logs and backups can have separate retention. Revoking a connection does not delete its audit history or your Firebase account.

Rate-limit records use keyed hashes of account or source identifiers. Hosting, authentication and service infrastructure can process connection metadata under Google’s policies.

3. Information on your computer

Kindred documents, calendar notes, preferences and pinned shortcuts are stored locally. Kindred’s own document and calendar features do not upload them to this website. Removed documents may remain in local recovery storage. Other installed apps, backup tools or synchronization services may handle your files separately.

The Debian desktop uses local PocketSphinx voice recognition and local speech playback. Development Linux, Mac, Windows and Android versions also implement optional OpenRouter voice; physical audio and live-provider acceptance differ by platform. When that feature is used: when you choose Send recording, your recording and up to two recent spoken questions are sent to OpenRouter’s OpenAI voice model. Recent recordings stay in app memory until cleared or the app closes; Kindred does not save them to the document store. Spoken replies are played locally.

4. Optional online assistant

Local settings and supported Home shortcut commands work without an online API key. The released Linux download uses NVIDIA. Development versions offer NVIDIA, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Google Gemini and Anthropic. When you configure a provider’s API key and send an online message, Kindred sends your message, recent conversation and the context needed for its tools to your selected provider. OpenRouter routes requests to the selected model’s provider. Your API key is used to authenticate the request and is stored in your local Kindred configuration or supplied through an environment variable; it is not submitted to this website.

Do not send secrets or sensitive personal information in chat. Your selected provider and, when using OpenRouter, the model provider handle processing and retention under their own policies and your service agreement. NVIDIA’s privacy policy applies when using NVIDIA. You can stop a request, clear the local conversation, or remove your configured key. Clearing local chat does not delete information already received by a provider.

5. Weather, updates and other apps

Weather sends the city name you search to Open-Meteo’s geocoding service and the selected place’s coordinates to its forecast service. Requests also expose connection information to the provider. See Open-Meteo’s terms and privacy information.

Update checks contact this website for release metadata; update downloads contact GitHub. Debian and Flatpak package operations contact their configured repositories. Browsing, email and third-party apps send information to the services you choose under those services’ policies.

6. Your choices, retention and security

You can use local desktop features without configuring online AI, choose whether to use voice input, and change automatic Kindred update settings. Manage third-party cookies and saved sign-ins in your browser. Local data remains until you remove it or the relevant app removes it; backups and recovery copies may remain separately.

We use HTTPS for the public website and download links. No system can guarantee complete security. Keep backups, protect your device and API key, and install security updates. This website’s code does not sell personal information or send it to advertising systems.

7. Questions and privacy requests

Depending on where you live and what information is processed, applicable law may give you rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of personal information, or complain to a privacy regulator. Contact the project through the website issue tracker to ask how to make a privacy request. Start with a general request for a private contact method; do not post personal information in a public issue. Requests about a provider’s own records should also be directed to that provider.

8. Updates to this policy

We will revise this page if the website’s or desktop’s described data practices change. Review the revision date before downloading. This policy describes the current implementation and does not authorize future collection beyond applicable law.
