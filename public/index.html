A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s make
it clear.

Nothing to rush. Here’s what to know before you start.

What if I press the wrong thing?

On this website, choose Home to find your way back. The practice area uses sample items and has Undo. On the Kindred desktop, removed Kindred documents are kept in Recently removed so you can restore them. Other apps have their own controls. If something is unfamiliar, pause before confirming a change.

Can someone help me get started?

Yes. Invite someone you trust to explore the practice area with you. If you decide to install Kindred, work through the installation guide together and back up your files first. You can take a break at any point before starting installation.

Will it work on my computer?

Kindred supports 64-bit Intel and AMD computers. The installer needs Internet access and Secure Boot turned off. Wi-Fi, sound, printers and other hardware vary by device. See the installation guide before changing your computer.

Will installing it erase my files?

Installing a new OS can erase the disk you select. Back up your files first. The installer asks you to choose a disk and confirm. Updating an existing Kindred installation keeps its documents and settings.

Does the assistant need the Internet?

Text size, contrast and Home pinning commands work locally. Online answers require Internet and an NVIDIA API key. Your message and recent conversation are sent to NVIDIA. Replies may be mistaken. Voice recognition runs locally; review the words before sending.

Are these official desktop apps?

YouTube, Instagram and the other familiar online services open their official websites. They are web apps, not official Linux desktop packages. Flathub offers additional Linux apps; check each publisher and permissions.

How do I update an older Kindred?

Older 0.5.1 and earlier installations need the one-time upgrade on the download page to accept the new public download source. After upgrading, automatic Kindred updates can check daily without restarting your session.

Can I use a Linux terminal?

Yes. Settings → Developer mode adds a normal-user Linux terminal to Home and Advanced settings. It is off by default. The assistant cannot enable developer mode or execute terminal commands.
