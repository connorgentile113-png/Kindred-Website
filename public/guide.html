Try a gentle practice

ONE STEP AT A TIME

A fresh start,
with a little care.

Read through these steps before you begin.
Installation can erase the disk you select.

IN THIS GUIDE

1. Get ready2. Download & verify3. Make your USB4. Start the installer5. Settle in
01 / PREPARE

Keep the things that matter.

Back up your photos, documents and other files to a separate drive or a service you trust. Check that you can open the backup.

You need a 64-bit Intel or AMD computer, a USB drive that fits the installer, and Internet access while installing. ARM computers are not supported by this image. Hardware support varies.

Using an existing Kindred? Follow the application upgrade instructions instead of reinstalling.
02 / DOWNLOAD

Get your installer.

Download the ISO and its checksum from the download page. Keep both files in the same folder. Verify the SHA-256 value before writing the USB.

sha256sum -c kindred-os-0.5.2-amd64.iso.sha256

On Linux, the result should say OK. On another system, use a checksum tool to compare the expected SHA-256 shown on the download page. If the values differ, download the installer again.

03 / MAKE A USB

A small drive. A new beginning.

Use a USB image-writing tool you trust. Select the Kindred ISO and write it as a disk image to your USB drive. Check the selected drive carefully: writing the image erases that USB drive.

When writing finishes, safely eject the USB. Leave your backup drive disconnected while installing so it cannot be selected by mistake.

04 / INSTALL

Take it one choice at a time.

  1. Turn off Secure Boot in your computer’s firmware settings.
  2. Insert the Kindred USB, restart, and select the USB in your computer’s boot menu. The boot-menu key varies by manufacturer.
  3. Choose Install Kindred OS. Follow the network and administrator setup prompts.
  4. Select the disk for Kindred. Read the disk-erasure confirmation carefully before continuing.
  5. Finish installation, remove the USB when prompted, and restart.
If you’re unsure about the disk or a firmware setting, stop here and ask someone you trust.

This installer is not a live desktop preview. You can inspect its boot menu in a virtual machine before changing a physical computer. Chromebook firmware varies and may require additional preparation.

05 / SETTLE IN

Make yourself at home.

Start with the six activities. Open Apps to pin your favourites. Choose larger text or stronger contrast in Settings. Help opens your assistant.

Sign in to online services with your own accounts. For online assistant questions, add your NVIDIA API key under Voice & chat options. Simple settings and pinning commands work without that key.

Check your Wi-Fi, sound and other devices. Keep your backup until you’re happy everything you need is working.
